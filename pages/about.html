Our Principles

What we will not compromise on

Verification before visibility

No business goes live without GSTIN, MSME/Udyam and address verification. Unverified listings never receive a badge — and never rank above verified ones.

Transparent commercials

MOQ, price bands, capacity and lead time are structured fields, not free-text promises. Buyers compare facts.

Payment protection

Escrow and a structured dispute process mean neither side has to rely on goodwill alone to close a deal.

Built for Bharat's scale

Tamil to Hindi, UPI to LC, kirana to conglomerate. The product is designed around how Indian businesses actually buy and sell.

Neutral by design

We are a marketplace, not a reseller. We never take ownership of stock or compete with the businesses we list.

Free to start

Listing is free, always. We charge only when a business chooses to buy more visibility, so growth is never paywalled at the entry point.
