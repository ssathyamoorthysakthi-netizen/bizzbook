Common Questions

Pricing FAQs

Is listing really free?

Yes. Every business gets a free listing with a verified badge, up to 25 products and unlimited buyer enquiries. We never charge buyers to search or contact a supplier.

How does lead pricing work?

Paid plans buy visibility and quality, not raw volume: priority placement, AI-matched RFQs and deeper analytics. You are never charged per lead click.

Can I change plans later?

Anytime. Upgrades apply immediately and downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle. Yearly plans save roughly two months.

What is the escrow fee?

A flat 1.5% platform fee on the transaction value, charged only on completed orders. No payment gateway charges on top.
