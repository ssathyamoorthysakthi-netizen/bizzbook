Frequently Asked Questions

How long does verification take?

Document checks complete within two working days. Businesses needing an on-site factory visit take 5–7 working days.

Is it free to list a business?

Yes. The Starter plan includes one listing, up to 25 products, a free verified badge and unlimited buyer enquiries.

Can buyers see supplier phone numbers?

Contact details are shared only after a buyer raises an enquiry, protecting both parties from spam.

How does escrow protect my payment?

Funds are held by BizBook and released to the supplier only after you inspect and accept the consignment.
