Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 September 2026. This policy explains what information BizBook collects, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices available to you.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information — name, business name, email address, mobile number, role, company profile, GSTIN and business address.
  • Verification documents — GST certificate, MSME/Udyam registration, incorporation documents, ISO and other certificates, and factory photographs where you submit them for verification.
  • Listing content — product names, specifications, MOQ, price bands, images, catalogues and service descriptions you publish.
  • Transaction records — enquiries, quotations, purchase orders, invoices, payment records, logistics references and messages exchanged between users.
  • Usage data — pages viewed, searches made, filters applied, device and browser type, IP address, referring page and interaction timestamps.
  • Communications — support tickets, call logs, and correspondence with our team.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and operate your account, listings, enquiries and orders.
  • To verify businesses and issue or revoke the BizBook Verified badge.
  • To match buyers with relevant suppliers and to route enquiries.
  • To operate escrow payments, invoicing and dispute resolution.
  • To detect, investigate and prevent fraud, abuse and prohibited listings.
  • To improve search relevance, product experience and platform performance.
  • To send service messages about your account, transactions and (where you have consented) relevant product updates.
  • To comply with legal, tax and regulatory obligations.

3. Sharing Your Information

We share information only as needed to operate the marketplace:

  • With other users — your business name, location, verification status and listing content are public. Your mobile number, email address and full address are shared with a counterparty only after either party raises an enquiry or places an order.
  • Service providers — hosting, payment gateways, logistics partners, SMS and email providers, analytics tools and verification vendors, under contract and only for the stated purpose.
  • Authorities — where required by law, court order, or a valid government notice.
  • Corporate transactions — in connection with a merger, acquisition or asset sale, with appropriate confidentiality safeguards.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

4. Verification of Third-Party Data

To display verification badges we query GST, MSME/Udyam and other public registries. This means information about your business — including registration status and registered address — may be displayed to users. If any of this is inaccurate, contact us and we will correct or remove it.

5. Cookies and Analytics

We use strictly necessary cookies to keep you signed in, remember your preferences and support security. We also use analytics cookies to understand aggregate usage. You can control non-essential cookies through your browser settings or our cookie preferences tool. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may prevent parts of the platform from working.

6. Data Retention

We retain account information for as long as your account is active. Transaction and tax records are retained for the period required under Indian tax and accounting law (typically 8 years). When you close your account, we anonymise or delete personal data that is no longer required, except where retention is legally required.

7. Data Security

We use encryption in transit and at rest, role-based access controls, audit logging, regular vulnerability assessments and independent security testing. No system is completely secure, but we continuously work to protect your data and will notify you and the relevant authorities of a breach as required by law.

8. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request to:

  • Access and obtain a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Withdraw consent for non-essential processing, including marketing communications.
  • Request deletion or erasure of your account and data.
  • Nominate someone to exercise your rights in the event of your death or incapacity.

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@bizbook.in. We will verify your identity before acting on the request and respond within 30 days.

9. Children's Information

BizBook is a business platform and is not intended for persons under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Grievance Officer

Mr. Rakesh Nair, Grievance Officer, BizBook Marketplace Pvt. Ltd., 4th Floor, Sankalp Square, Ahmedabad, Gujarat 380059. Email grievance@bizbook.in. Complaints are acknowledged within 48 hours and resolved within 7 working days.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be notified by email and in-product notice at least 15 days before they take effect. Your continued use of BizBook after that means you accept the revised policy.
